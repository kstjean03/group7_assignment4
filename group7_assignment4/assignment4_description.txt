DESCRIPTION: 
Our final animation shows a 2D solar system scene. The sun is placed in the center of the screen, and all eight planets orbit around it at different speeds and distances. The animation runs continuously in a loop.
Each planet rotates around the sun while also spinning on its own axis. Some planets have moons, and some have rings. Moons orbit their parent planets, and rings rotate or pulse around their planets. This demonstrates hierarchical transformations because moons depend on planets, and planets depend on the sun.
The background includes stars. Some stars stay in place and pulse to look like they are twinkling. Other stars move diagonally across the screen as shooting stars. Each star is made of a core and a glow, where the glow is a child object that animates separately.
We also included background space audio and a sound effect that plays when a shooting star appears.
